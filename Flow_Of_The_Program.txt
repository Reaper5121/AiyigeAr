Aiyige System Flow

Customer flow
1. Sign in and browse the mobile catalog, or customize a product with AR.
2. Add the selected products to checkout. An AR room capture may be attached as
   a reference; it does not create an order.
3. The backend prepares an official quotation from current product and price
   data. The app displays it for the customer to review and confirm.
4. If the customer agrees, they choose J&T or Lalamove. If they decline, `No,
   go back` returns them to where checkout began with their selection intact.
5. The customer selects a payment method, then reviews the complete order
   summary.
6. On final submission, the backend links the quotation to one order and creates
   the payment record. Repeating a submission for the same quotation returns
   its existing records.
7. The customer can review order updates in the app.

Staff flow
1. Staff manages inventory and records order preparation and delivery progress.
2. Staff records the handoff to the courier selected by the customer.
3. Staff may print the quotation from the order record. Staff does not choose or
   book the courier and does not attach an external courier quotation.

Data and integration notes
- J&T and Lalamove are external delivery choices; this project records the
  customer's selection and delivery status, but does not book either courier.
- Supabase synchronization is optional and requires server-side credentials.
- Payment request review is a separate operational record; it does not create
  an order unless the customer completes checkout.
